> 摘要: 水库多目标优化调度是一个复杂的系统优化问题，属于高维、多约束、非线性问题的优化领域。智能优化公众号：数学建模老哥

算法在求解水库多目标优化调度时，存在非劣解较少、分布不均匀及不收敛等问题，基于上述问题，提出一种求解

新思路，通过加入精英保留机制并用拥挤距离来维持外部精英集改进多目标差分进化算法。建立了多目标水库优

化调度模型，并以综合利用的漳河水库多目标优化调度为例进行了求解，结果表明该算法能够得到分布较为均匀

的非劣解集，证明方法在水库多目标优化调度中是有效的。

关键词: 多目标差分进化算法; 外部精英集; 拥挤距离; 水库优化调度; 漳河水库

Abstract: The multi-objective optimal operation of reservoirs is a complex system optimization，and it belongs to the scope of high-

dimension，multi-constraint，and nonlinear optimization．The intelligent optimization algorithm has the shortcomings of less non-inferior

solutions，uneven distribution and non-convergence when solving the multi-objective optimal operation of reservoirs．Based on the above

problems，this paper proposes a new idea to improve the multi-objective differential evolution algorithm by adding the elite retention

mechanism and maintaining the external elite set with the crowding distance，establishes the multi-objective optimal operation model for

reservoirs，and applies it for realizing the multi-objective optimal operation of Zhanghe Ｒeservoir．The results show that the algorithm can

obtain non-inferior solution set with uniform distribution，which proves that it is effective in multi-objective optimal operation of

reservoirs．

Keywords: multi-objective differential evolution algorithm; external elite set; crowding distance; optimal operation of reservoirs; Zhanghe

Ｒeservoir
