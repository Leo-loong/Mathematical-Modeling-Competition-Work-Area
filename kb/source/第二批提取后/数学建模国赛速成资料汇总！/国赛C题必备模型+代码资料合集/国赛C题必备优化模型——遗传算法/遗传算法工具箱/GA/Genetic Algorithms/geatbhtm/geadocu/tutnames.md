## 5 Naming conventions

 Throughout the toolbox the functions are named according the following

 conventions.

 High level script functions:

 scr*.m (set parameter for a special problem, used as demonstrations)

 High level toolbox algorithms:

 tbx*.m (define a special genetic algorithm of the toolbox)

 Objective functions:

 obj*.m (implement a special problem)

 Simulation function:

 sim*.m (called by a corresponding objective function, second

 part of name is identical to second part of corresponding objective

 function)

 Application specific plot functions:

 plot*.m (second part of name is identical to second part of

 corresponding objective function)

 Low level selection function:

 sel*.m

 Low recombination function:

 rec*.m

 Low level crossover function:

 xov*.m

 Low level mutation function:

 mut*.m

 Low level reinsertion function:

 reins*.m
