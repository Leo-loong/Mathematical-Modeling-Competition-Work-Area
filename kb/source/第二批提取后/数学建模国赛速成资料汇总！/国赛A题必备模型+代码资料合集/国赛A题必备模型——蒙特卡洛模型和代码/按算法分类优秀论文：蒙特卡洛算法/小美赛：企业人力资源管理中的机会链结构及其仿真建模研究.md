Abstract: Human resource management is an important part for the modern

enterprise development. However, there are so many problems existing in today’s

human resources of China and how to manage human resources effectively is still a

very practical subject. In this paper we have established an Opportunity Chain Model

which is based on the subject, focusing on solving the human resource management

issues in the enterprise and combined the small-world networks and the Monte Carlo

simulation methods to discover the law of movement for persons within the enterprise

for the purpose of solving the human resources consumption problems in the next five

years.

For the problem (1),through the comparison of the "freeze" and "flow" system, we

> found a continuous flowing of job opportunity and abandonment of the existing work公众号：数学建模老哥

are the two necessary conditions to maintain the opportunity chain. And when

comparing the worker’s salary change of position flow, we can find that each person’s

salary level is increased.

For the problem (2), we understand the movement of persons within the enterprise

before and after the change by introducing the main ideas and methods of modeling

from the perspective of complex adaptive systems, and get the solution to determine

the number of candidates by using the computer simulations to simulate individual

behavior in this complex process work queue.

In the paper we constructed an Opportunity Chain Model, defined the state of the

body through the conversion and the "small world" network model to describe the

rules of conduct between the subject, and got a simple simulation modeling

framework which could be used to simulate the flowing process. Thus, it is possible to

set dynamically extensible rules and adjust the model parameters, which provides a

convenient simulation environment for the researchers. Then according to the number

of "vacancy", through Monte Carlo simulation to generate random Numbers,

determine the number of applicants. It’s found that when there’s a vacancy, the

enterprise need 10 candidates, two openings, need 20 candidates. Finally, according to

the above results, we can determine the change of the human resource cost over the

next five years.

attractive jobs. And this causes the original position vacancy, providing others with

new jobs.The position vacancy, fill-up and exchange that resulted from internal

employee’s turnover, and which also results in a series of career opportunities—

#### 1.2 Why do we use the “chain”?

Human resource management is a theoretical, technical and practical work. But

there are many problems, like the unreasonable human resource structure, serious

brain drain from the work, less investment in human capital, backward training

system and so on, in most companies in our country, when compared with the

developed western countries’ standardize human resource management.

The application of the “chain” can help companies mining the relation between

the internal positions. And we can solve problems, like the brain drain, imbalance

between supply and demand, too old methods in management, which may appear in

the work of human resource management, by analyzing the impact of career mobility,

so that it can help companies excavate potential talents, improve the level of human

resource management.

1.3 The analysis of the structure and function of opportunity

#### chain

Opportunity chain is made up of vacancy chain, mobile chain.

Vacancy chain is formed by the position vacancy when employees leave from the

work. Position vacancy can affect how the company work, so the HR department will

bring in new employees to make sure that the company work well.

<!-- 第 7 页 -->

Mobile chain refers to the career mobile chain that resulted from the predecessor’

s left and job seekers’ involved. With the development of the world, there is more

and more space in career development, more and more alternative opportunities when

seeking jobs. And people who have more capabilities are willing to pursue better

career. So the mobile chain of career will always inject fresh blood to the human

resource market, companies finding out the right employees, job-seekers owning

better rewards and development for career.

#### 1.4 Introduction about complex adaptive system which based

#### on the subject

The main idea of CAS(complex adaptive system) is, the complexity of CAS is

originated in the adaptability of the subject, the interaction between the subject and

the environment, and other subjects, constantly changes themselves, also the

environment. New structure and phenomenon of overall level and the more complex

behavior will be highlighted through the interaction of the subjects.

Since the 1990s, simulation modeling technology which based on the

> subject(Agent) is widely used in research in the field of social economy and ecology,公众号：数学建模老哥

etc. This is a "bottom-up" object-oriented simulation modeling method, using the

latest research achievements in the field of artificial intelligence and computer science,

directly at the micro level simulation system of the main body, and based on the rules

of the interaction between subject and subject, and thus the overall behavior of the

system.

#### 2.1 How do we approximate the changes in human resource

#### consumption over the next five years?

- Opportunity chain modeling and simulation mainly achieve three parts, the

subject modeling (real-time data objects), the definition of simulation rule and

the indication of simulation results.

- The subject modeling (real-time data objects): Analyzing the data base and

research report, extracting the relevant information, adopting the OOM

(Object oriented method), the subject of the program is created by modeling.

- Definition of simulation rule: We can define the rule of emulational subject’s

state switching and its behavior by defining some indexes, for example,

whether the subject is at the age of retire, whether there is some space to let

its capability improve, whether its capability satisfy the vacant career’s need.

- Indication of simulation results: Let it come true when search the subject’s

career, age, capability and so on.

#### 2.2 How to describe the change of people’s change?

Due to the ability of people has obvious group, so we choose the “small world”

model, which can reflect the influence of people’s ability, to study the impact among

company’s internal person through their communication, so that we can know how it

influence their ability.

#### 2.3 How do we approximate the cost of human resource?

The cost of human resource includes selecting, training, employing, circulating

and so on. So we will make an investigation on these aspects.

1) Selecting cost: When the company appears career vacancy, we need to make a

supplement for the position by selecting job-seekers or promoting internal

employees, and the cost in this process can be a constant value in some cases.

2) Training cost: The employees need training when he or she is in a new company

or new position. The cost is inversely proportional to the subject’s ability when

we use the basic model.

3) Employing cost: When the new employees participate in the work, the company

> needs to pay salary and welfare to them which can satisfy each other.公众号：数学建模老哥

4) Circulating cost: When someone retired, the company will pay a severance.

#### 2.4 How to consider the question that how many job-seekers

#### will meet the needs of employers and employees at the same

#### time?

When there is a career vacancy in an enterprise, in order not to affect the profits

and competitiveness of the enterprise, it is necessary to go to the talent market for

recruitment to make a supplement. From the perspective of the business, it hopes the

vacancies can get a reasonable addition, and recruitment costs as little as possible as

well.

#### 2.5 What if there is no data available?

The adjustable parameters of the model can be divided into two categories, one is

used to reflect the property and the mutual influence of the subjects, another is used to

control the length of the model’s simulation cycle on the macro level. The subjective

indicators are quantified by quantitative variables. The initial values of parameters is

based on the summary, abstract or other simplified means of existed research

materials.

Now suppose that a enterprise composed of 50 people, enterprises have five

positions, salary is the same when in the same position and the salary is proportional

to the position’s level.
