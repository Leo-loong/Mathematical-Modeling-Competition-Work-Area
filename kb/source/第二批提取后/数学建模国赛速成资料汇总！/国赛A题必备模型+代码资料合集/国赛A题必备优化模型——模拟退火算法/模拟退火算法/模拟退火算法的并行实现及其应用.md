ABSTRACT
The simulated annealing algorithm was modified and realized on a home2made parallel super computer system THNPSC21.
The program was applied to the data analyses of two different physical domains : Convergent Beam Electron Diffraction and Auger
Electron Spectroscopy. The good results obtained from these two domains indicated that the program is of high2performance and is
applicable to various research regions. The source code is available free of charge from the authors.
