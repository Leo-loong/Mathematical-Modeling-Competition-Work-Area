LI Han, WU Zhenzhen, ZHANG Xuexue
(Beijing Wuzi University, Beijing 101149, China)

Abstract: By analyzing the relevant factors affecting the logistics demand in Beijing, this paper constructed an index system for its
forecasting. Through the BP neural network and GM(1,1) method, a combination forecasting model for the logistics demand in Beijing was
then established, and based on the statistical data of the city from the past 20 years, the logistics demand in the next five years was forecast,
obtaining total logistics demand volume figures and the relevant pattern of change. On the basis of the foregoing, an effective path was
proposed to promote the development of the logistics industry in Beijing.
