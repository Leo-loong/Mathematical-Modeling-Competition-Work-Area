Abstract: To realize the reasonable planning of vehicle routing problem，reduce the traveling distance of
vehicle and the cost of distribution，an improved whale optimization algorithm is proposed to solve the
mathematical model of vehicle routing problem．This paper chooses the lowest cost and shortest path as
the objective function，encodes the whale position as the vehicle number and the vehicle path order，and
realizes the optimal planning of multi-objective logistics distribution vehicle path through the improved
whale optimization algorithm．The results show that compared with WOA，PSO and GA，the improved
whale optimization algorithm can reduce the distribution cost and distance，and provide a new method for
vehicle route planning．
